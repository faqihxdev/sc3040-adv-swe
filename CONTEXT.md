# UseEatNow

Shared household ingredients, cooking and food-waste records.

## Language

**Recorded quantity**: The amount currently held by the household, including portions reserved for cooking.

**Reserved quantity**: A portion held for a member's active cooking session.

**Available quantity**: Recorded quantity minus all active reserved portions.

**Cooking session**: A member's selected recipe and the portions reserved until confirmation, cancellation or expiry.

**Actual use**: The amount consumed when a cooked meal is confirmed.
