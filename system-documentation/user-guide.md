# UseEatNow quick guide

Private demo: use the accounts shared by the team. Local accounts/setup are in
the [app README](../source-code/README.md). Local and hosted passwords differ.

## UC1: Find meals

- Cook → Find meals. Choose people, preferences and available staples; deselect anything absent.
- Next → inspect a recipe → Cook this. Selection leaves stock unchanged.

## UC2: Inventory

- Add ingredient → Manual or Photo. Review name, stock band and required expiry; preview, then confirm.
- Photo recognition creates editable drafts. Enter stock and expiry yourself.
- Edit/remove from each row. Activity & undo reverses your latest safe edit.

## UC3: Finish cooking

- Complete the steps → I've finished cooking.
- Mark used/discarded independently and set remaining stock. Preview → Confirm meal & stock.
- Untouched items retain stock. Outcomes are saved only after confirmation.

## UC4: Insights

- View recorded meal/use/discard events and trends. View chart data opens a table.
- Group by week uses Monday-start Singapore weeks. Insights cover recorded activity only.

## UC5: Access/preferences

- Sign in. Local signup/recovery are available outside private-demo mode.
- Household → Dietary exclusions → Save. Exclusions override preferences; check allergens yourself.

## UC6: Household/account

- Create or join with the owner's invite code. Owners can invite, remove members or transfer ownership; members can leave.
- Account controls appearance, sign out and typed account deletion. Owners first transfer ownership or delete the household.

## Conflicts and safety

- Conflicting write: reload current state and review again. Retry the same failed confirmation.
- AI failure: preferences remain saved. Use Try again or Change preferences.
- Past-date/depleted items remain in records and are excluded from recommendations.
- Stock bands are approximate; analytics count events. Check real quantities and food safety before cooking.

## Demo reset

- Account → Reset to seed is available only to the allowlisted demo owner.
- Reset replaces household inventory/history while retaining accounts, members, preferences and AI usage.
